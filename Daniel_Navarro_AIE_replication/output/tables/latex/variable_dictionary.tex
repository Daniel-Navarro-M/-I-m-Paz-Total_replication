\caption{Variable dictionary for the aggregate panel.}

\begin{tabular}{l c}
\hline
column name & description \\
\hline
case\_id & Study case identifier: buenaventura, arauca, tumaco. \\
case\_label & Human-readable study case label. \\
spatial\_definition & Territorial scope of each study case. \\
month\_id & Calendar month in YYYY-MM format. \\
month\_start & First day of the month for plotting. \\
total\_events & Aggregate monthly count of all included observations across the merged Excel sources. \\
treatment & Peace talks active in that case-month (1=yes, 0=no). \\
clash\_dummy & Dummy equal to 1 in months with active armed-group dispute or clashes in the territorial case. \\
gold\_price & Monthly gold price matched by month from gold.xlsx. \\
oil\_price & Monthly oil price matched by month from oil.xlsx. \\
cocaine\_price & Monthly cocaine price interpolated from yearly values. \\
month\_index & Sequential month index used for panel ordering. \\
